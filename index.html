✦ HOW TO WALK

THE COVENANT, IN SIX BREATHS

1

the forge assigns you an empty seat — one of the 1,131 the contract carved but never minted. you can't own it; you walk it. hold it through five duels and it's yours in the ledger.

2

each era, one enemy stands. it announces its next two moves at the right of its bar: ⚔️ 9 = hit that hard, 💥 CHR its next hit doubles, 🌧 it curses your deck, ✚ it heals, 🌀 it copies your last card.

3

you get 3 ⚡ a turn and a hand of 5 cards. every card costs ⚡ (the blue circle). click the card to play it. ⚔️ light = damage · 🛡 = shield that soaks hits · ✚ = you heal.

4

like your cards too much? END TURN without playing anything and patience ⏳ grows (up to 3) — next turn each patience is +1 ⚡. stillness is strength… but the enemy still moves. pick your moment.

5

played cards fade to the pile and come back around the bend. win a duel → choose 1 of 3 new sayings and rest. lose → the seat stays empty. nothing breaks — take another.

6

read the cards before you play them — every card is a real toadgod saying and it means what it says: darkness hurts you, the stone waits, a hush silences. walk wise.
