# THE EMPTY SEATS

4,000 seats were carved. 2,869 minted. **1,131 stay empty — forever.**

A turn-based lore duel game for Tobyworld. You take one of the 1,131 empty
seats (real numbers, derived from the onchain archive in `data/empty-seats.json`)
and walk it through five duels — one per epoch:

| Epoch | Boss | Its scroll |
|---|---|---|
| I | THE SACRED WAR | “war and death, they solve not a bit” — T053 |
| II | THE DOUBTERS | “clones may mimic, doubters jeer, fear mongers near” — T047 |
| III | THE STORM | “storms thicken, toads breathe” — T107 |
| IV | THE MIMIC | “inimitable, no mimic, no clone” — T034 |
| V | THE FADE | “there is no light without darkness” — T071 |

Every card is a **real toadgod saying with its real date** — the code of the
game is the lore itself: “patience 🔵 strength” grows your energy when you hold
your hand; “there is no light without darkness” wounds you to strike hard;
“the stone does not bend in wind” blocks and echoes; “no hype needed” quietly
burns away the DOUBT the doubters curse you with.

- Boss portraits are cuts from the canonical paintings (deed #11, #68, #19,
  #1073, #16) — copies only, the masters are never modified.
- Seats you hold are remembered in your browser (per-viewer), and every saying
  you meet lands in THE CODEX.
- `#selftest` plays a whole walk by itself, headless-safe, and writes the
  result to `#selftest-out` (`#selftest<N>` freezes after N ticks).
- `#selfd` plays THE DAILY COVENANT and writes the day's seat + share line —
  run it twice on one dawn and the output matches letter for letter.

## THE DAILY COVENANT

One walk a dawn, the same for every frog. Day 0 = 2026-10-27 (`DAWN 1`); the
day's seed (`covenant-day-<day>` through the fnv bloodline) fixes the seat,
the deal and the whole boss sequence with ONE mulberry stream consumed in
order — so all viewers of one morning walk the identical covenant. The copy
of the walk works on every end screen.

## THE KEEPERS

You never walk alone — one of the three keepers walks it with you:

| Keeper | Blessing |
|---|---|
| 🟦 OG THE ELDER | every strike deals +2 light |
| 🌫 HUSH | every duel opens quiet — the boss's first move dissolves; brings a HUSH card |
| 🟨 SATOBY | every heal drinks +2 deeper |
